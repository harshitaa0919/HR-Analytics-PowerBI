\# Project Overview



\## HR Analytics Dashboard



This project is a self-driven HR analytics project created using Microsoft Power BI. The objective of the project was to explore employee data and identify meaningful patterns related to employee attrition, demographics, salary, job roles, education, and tenure.



The dashboard was developed from a raw HR dataset. The analysis was performed independently in Power BI by exploring the available data, creating relevant calculations and visualizations, and presenting the findings through an interactive dashboard.



\## Project Objective



The main objective of this project was to understand employee attrition and identify the workforce characteristics associated with employee turnover.



The analysis focuses on:



\- Overall employee count and attrition

\- Attrition rate

\- Employee age distribution

\- Salary levels

\- Years spent at the company

\- Attrition by gender

\- Attrition by education

\- Attrition by job role

\- Attrition by salary range

\- Attrition by years at the company

\- Department-level analysis



\## Tools Used



\- Microsoft Power BI

\- Power BI Data Modeling

\- DAX

\- Data Visualization

\- Business Analysis



\## Dataset



The project uses a raw HR employee dataset containing employee-level information such as demographics, job roles, salary, education, department, job satisfaction, and employment history.



The dataset was explored and analyzed in Power BI to identify patterns and generate insights related to employee attrition.



\## Dashboard



The dashboard provides an interactive view of key HR metrics and attrition patterns.



Key metrics presented include:



\- Total Employees

\- Attrition Count

\- Attrition Rate

\- Average Age

\- Average Salary

\- Average Years at Company



The dashboard also provides visual analysis of attrition across different employee characteristics.



\## Approach



The project followed a simple analytical workflow:



1\. Imported the raw HR dataset into Power BI.

2\. Explored the available employee attributes and metrics.

3\. Created relevant calculations and measures.

4\. Analyzed employee attrition across different dimensions.

5\. Designed visualizations to communicate the findings.

6\. Combined the analysis into an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard.



\## Outcome



The final dashboard provides a consolidated view of employee attrition and workforce characteristics, making it easier to identify patterns across age groups, job roles, salary ranges, education, gender, and employee tenure.



This project helped strengthen practical skills in Power BI, data visualization, exploratory analysis, and communicating business insights through dashboards.
